01 — The lot

The lot is the product.

You are not buying a seat. You rent the screen, the parking space and the sound for one night. The movie is whatever you brought.

The screen

Thirty feet of canvas, lit from a booth at the back of the lot.

The lot

Your car, parked facing the screen, with room to stretch out between reels.

The sound

Audio plays over FM. Tune your car radio to the lot channel and keep the windows down.

02 — A night

How a night works.

  1. 1

    Reserve

    Pick a date and a plan. We hold the lot for your car for ninety minutes.

  2. 2

    Arrive

    Drive in, park facing the screen, and hand your reel to the booth.

  3. 3

    Project

    Your film runs on the big screen while the radio carries the sound.

  4. 4

    Lantern

    At intermission the marquee gives way to lanterns. Stretch, talk, and wait for the next reel.

03 — Your reel

Bring your reel.

8 mm, Super 8, 16 mm, or a digital file on a drive. We check the format when you book, so the night starts on time.
