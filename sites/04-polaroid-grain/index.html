How it works

Three steps to an afternoon that never happened.

  1. 01

    Choose a year

    Pick 1972, 1975 or 1978. Each year has its own film stock and its own sky.

  2. 02

    Choose a light

    Winter, summer or rain. The light decides the grain, the halation and how the colors drift.

  3. 03

    Receive your prints

    Three instant photographs in a worn paper frame, developed by hand and posted to your door.

The rolls

Three years. Three kinds of light.

Each roll is exposed in its own decade of light, with its own grain and its own drift of color. Point at a roll to see how it develops.

Roll 1972 selected: winter light.

The lab

We develop what the sun never saw.

Each roll is wound onto a steel reel in a room with no windows, then bathed in developer until an ordinary afternoon appears where none happened. The chemistry is slow on purpose.

Developer leaves stains, bubbles and a drift of color that creeps in from the corners. We keep them. A memory with no stain looks too clean to trust.
