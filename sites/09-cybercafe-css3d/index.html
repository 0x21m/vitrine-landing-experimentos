02 / Billing

You pay for the wait

Every booth is rented by the minute. The line stays busy for the whole session, so you never get through and nobody gets through to you.

DOT-MATRIX PRINTER · LINE 1
  1. 01

    Dial

    The modem starts the handshake and the tone plays. It does not finish, and that is the point.

  2. 02

    Wait

    Billing runs by the minute while the line stays busy. No ringing, no notifications, no news.

  3. 03

    Leave

    When your time runs out, the line hangs up on its own. Nothing was sent. Your receipt is the only thing you keep.

03 / Booths

Three ways to wait

  • HANDSHAKETRYING...

    Handshake Booth

    Thirty minutes of the modem trying to connect. The tone rings and never resolves. Nobody answers, and nobody hangs up.

  • MAILBOX0 SENT

    Mailbox Booth

    Write a message on a glowing screen. It stays there. It is never sent, and no one can reply.

  • EMPTY0 USERS

    Empty Chat Room

    A chat room with a blinking cursor and no one else in it. You can type for as long as the line lets you.
