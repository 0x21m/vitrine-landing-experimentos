Process

How it works

  1. 1

    Dictate.

    Say the letter out loud to the typist across the desk. Do not stop to correct yourself.

  2. 2

    Type.

    The letter goes onto the machine once, with no backspace and no second draft.

  3. 3

    File.

    The original is numbered, dated and locked in the drawer. You take the carbon home.

Demonstration

The machine

A 1964 desk machine, one key at a time. The carriage moves as you scroll, and the letter fills in behind it.

Dear Ruth, I never said it properly, so I am typing it now, one key at a time. The kettle is still on the shelf where you left it. I kept the receipt too. You do not have to answer. I only needed the whole thing down, to the last full stop. Yours, M.
