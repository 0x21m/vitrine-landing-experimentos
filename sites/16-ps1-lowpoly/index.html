02 — What it is

25 minutes, on purpose.

Dorsal Hour rents you one 25-minute nap.

Each capsule is a cardboard sleep room the size of a crib: a foam mat, an eye mask, a 25-minute clock, and a memory card with an opening jingle.

We park the van outside. You lie down. The clock starts. The van takes the capsule back when the clock stops.

You pay per nap. No membership, no lobby.
