Process

How the tape arrives

  1. 001

    You speak

    Dictate the message you cannot say out loud yet. Take your time. We keep the words as you said them.

  2. 002

    We record it

    A narrator from our house reads it onto cassette tape in one take, then writes the label by hand.

  3. 003

    It waits

    The tape stays in its case until the date printed on the box. Then it goes out by post.

Step two

Before you order

Questions about the tape

Can the tape be sent back?

Yes, unopened, within 14 days of delivery. Once the date on the box has passed, the tape is yours to keep.

Can the delay be shortened?

Not after payment. You can change the delay once before you pay, and the label is written again with the new date.

Who narrates the tape?

A narrator from our house reads every tape. The words stay yours; only the voice is ours.
