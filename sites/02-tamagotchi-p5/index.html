06 · Firmware

Three menu steps, no buttons.

How the counter works. Use the arrow keys to move through the menu.

Step 1 of 3

Sense

The screen watches the pointer. If nothing moves over it for a while, the screen assumes nobody is looking, and the counter starts.

Step 2 of 3

Count

Each stretch of absence adds to the hour counter. The Lull grows with the count. Past 48 hours, a red flag tells you it is hungry.

Step 3 of 3

Reset

A pointer moving over the screen, or a tap on it, sets the counter to zero. The Lull shrinks and the screen flashes once to wake it.
