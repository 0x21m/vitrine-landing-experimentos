01 — The problem

Five people, five sheets.

Every meeting ends with five true statements that never line up into one decision. The disagreement is not the problem. Nobody can see where the disagreements overlap.

02 — How a session runs

Sixty minutes, one projector.

  1. 01 · Write

    Each person writes one position on one sheet. Short, in marker, with no notes on anyone else’s sheet.

  2. 02 · Stack

    All sheets go on the projector, one above another, under the same light.

  3. 03 · Read

    Only what shows through every layer is written into the record. The rest stays on the glass.

03 — Register

The record only accepts what lines up.

When the sheets are stacked, their registration marks have to meet. Each decision goes onto a record sheet, with the names of everyone who signed it.
