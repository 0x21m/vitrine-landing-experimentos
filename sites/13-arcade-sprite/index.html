01 — What we sell

We charge you a quarter to watch nothing happen.

We rent out a cabinet that never plays a game.

One coin buys ninety seconds of a ship that holds still.

You leave with a receipt for the time you did not spend playing.

02 — How it works

Three steps. None of them is play.

  1. Insert coin

    Press the button and the coin drops into the slot.

  2. Wait

    The ship sits in the middle of the screen for ninety seconds. Nothing happens.

  3. Take the receipt

    When the clock runs out, the printer ejects a slip with the time you spent not playing.

03 — The cabinet

A green tube, a plywood frame, one ship.

The screen is a phosphor tube behind an acrylic panel. The only sprite on it is a ship with its engines off. Move your pointer over the screen and its cockpit light blinks. The ship does not move.
